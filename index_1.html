Forehead

GAME

Phone up. Guess the word. Don't say it.

How to play

Pick your categories, choose how many players, and hit start. Whoever is up holds the phone flat against their forehead with the screen facing the room. Everyone else shouts hints. The person holding it guesses.

Tilt the phone face down when they get it and face up to skip, or leave tilt switched off and let someone tap the buttons instead. When the timer runs out you get the full list of what you got and what you missed, then you pass it on.

Common questions

Tilt is not doing anything

Tilt needs to be switched on first, at the bottom of the setup screen. Once a turn starts, bring the phone back upright between words. The game waits for the phone to return near vertical before it will register the next tilt, which is what stops one flick from burning through three words.

There is no sound

Check the volume and the silent switch on the side of the phone. The game mixes with whatever else is playing, so music in the background is fine.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. On the setup screen, type them into "Your own words", one per line or separated by commas. They get shuffled in with whichever categories you picked. They stay on your phone and are saved for next time.

The same words keep coming up

The deck is shuffled at the start of each game and works through every word before repeating. If you have only one or two categories on, the pool is small. Turn on more categories, or tap "Surprise me" for a random six.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole word bank ships inside the app. It works on a plane, in a basement, or with the phone in airplane mode.

Does it work on iPad?

It installs on iPad and runs in iPhone compatibility mode. It is built and tested for iPhone.
