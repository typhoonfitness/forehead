Forehead Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Forehead Game does not collect any data about you. None. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is ever gathered in the first place.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting service, no third-party SDKs, and no account system. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or microphone.

Network access

The app makes no network requests of any kind. The entire word bank is bundled inside the app. It works with the phone in airplane mode, and nothing ever leaves the device because nothing is ever sent.

What is stored on your phone

So the app remembers your setup between sessions, the following is saved locally on your device using Apple's standard preferences storage:

  • Which categories you had selected
  • Any custom words you typed in
  • Player or team names you entered
  • Your mode, player count, round count, timer length, and tilt preference

This stays on your phone. It is not transmitted, not backed up to any server run by me, and not readable by anyone else. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Motion sensors

If you switch on tilt controls, the app reads the accelerometer while a turn is in progress to tell whether the phone is facing up or down. That reading is used in the moment to score the word and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Motion access is off unless you enable tilt, and the game is fully playable with the on-screen buttons instead.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
